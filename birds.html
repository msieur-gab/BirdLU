Bird syllables

Candidate calls for a bird-song alphabet, cut from nine xeno-canto recordings of great tits, blue tits and robins. B01–B16 are clean and clearly different from one another; R01–R03 are reserves. Press Play on a card to hear it.

Recordings are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: free for this non-commercial prototype with credit; commercial use needs each recordist's permission.
