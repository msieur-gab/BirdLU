# Bird voice credits

The bird voice (birds/voice/, and the sketches in birds/sketch/ and birds/synth/) is synthesized from
models measured on the recordings below: each letter's pitch line, loudness and overtones were measured
on a real bird and sung again by a synthesizer (lab/synth.py). The recorded sketch versions use short
cuts of the recordings themselves.
