Letter by letter

Everything here comes straight out of the synthesizer: what you hear is what the phone will get. "D" is today's sound. ×2 and ×3: D repeated, as the vowels repeat. Sweep: one steep fall of an octave in 35 ms. Long: the same fall in 80 ms. Sweep ×3: the steep fall three times. Tap a button to hear it; tap it again to choose it (green). "Pair" plays both letters with your choices, one after the other.
