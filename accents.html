A bird for every accent

An accented letter is sung as its plain letter, then a short accent call: à = a + grave, ñ = n + tilde. If the call is lost, the letter is still there. Each call draws its mark: rising for acute, falling for grave, up and down for circumflex, two ticks for the dots, a waver for the tilde, a low note for the cedilla. Three real bird notes per accent: listen alone and in a word, and pick the one that sounds right. The six should also sound clearly different from each other.
