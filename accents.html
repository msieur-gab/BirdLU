A bird for every accent

An accented letter is sung as its plain letter, then a short accent call: à = a + grave, ñ = n + tilde. If the call is lost, the letter is still there. Every mark used in Europe, each on its own. Each call draws its mark (rising for acute, falling for grave, up and down for circumflex…), from real bird notes in the pitch range of the letters. Listen to the word without a call, then each candidate alone and in the word; pick one, or "none". The calls should also sound clearly different from each other.
