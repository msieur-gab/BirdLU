BirdLUSendListen

Letters sung as bird calls

Each letter has its own small call. One device sings a message, another listens and writes it back out. No network and no pairing: only sound.

SendType a message and play it out loud. Best on a computer with speakers. ListenLet the phone hear the calls and decode them into text.

Testing between two devices

  1. Open Send on the computer, keep Room at 0 and set the volume to about half.
  2. Open Listen on the phone and tap Start listening. Allow the microphone.
  3. Hold the phone about a metre from the speaker and press Play on the computer. Tick Repeat every 3 s to keep testing hands-free.
  4. Move further away, add Room echo, or talk nearby to find where it breaks.
