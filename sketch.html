A word is a phrase

A sound sketch, not an alphabet yet. Hear it synthesized (every note rebuilt from a model of a real bird) or from the recorded birds. A chiffchaff saying its name opens the message, and the same call reversed and slowing closes it. Each word is sung in one breath, and the bird pauses only between words. Vowels are real trills, cut whole so they keep the bird's own pace. Consonants are short notes leading in and out.

The score is the sound picture: pitch going up, time going right. The letters under it show which bird sings which letter.
